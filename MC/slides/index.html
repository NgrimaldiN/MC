Part 0 - Article

Zero variance Markov chain Monte Carlo for Bayesian estimators

A. Mira, R. Solgi and D. Imparato, Statistics and Computing, 2013.

Question of the paper

How can we reduce the Monte Carlo error of posterior averages without changing the target distribution?

Main idea used here

\[ \widetilde f(x) = f(x) + a^\top z(x), \qquad z(x)=-\frac12\nabla\log\pi(x) \]

  • The correction has expectation zero under the posterior.
  • The coefficient \(a\) can be estimated by a regression on the MCMC sample.
  • We apply this idea to posterior means in a Bayesian GARCH(1,1) model.
Keep this short orally: the paper is not about GARCH specifically; GARCH is the numerical example we reproduce. The essential message is that once we know the log-posterior gradient, we can build control variates whose expectation is zero and choose their coefficient by regression.
